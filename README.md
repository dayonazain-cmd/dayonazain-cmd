## About Me

I enjoy the entire lifecycle of a project — from a messy CSV file to a polished dashboard. Most of my work sits at the intersection of **statistical rigor** and **product thinking**: models are only useful if they actually ship and people use them. I'm particularly drawn to **time-series forecasting**, **portfolio optimization**, and **interactive dashboards**.

## Featured Projects

### 1. [Indonesia Multi-Asset Market Analytics](https://github.com/dayonazain-cmd/indonesia-financial-timeseries)
> An end-to-end Data Science project analyzing Indonesia and global financial markets (2010-2026).
*   **Key Features:** Data auditing, Machine Learning (XGBoost) for market direction, GARCH volatility modeling, and Markowitz Portfolio Optimization.
*   **Tech Stack:** Python, XGBoost, Statsmodels, Plotly, Streamlit.
*   **[View Live Dashboard](https://indonesia-financial-timeseries-mgu2o4muzttrslg9zaitjf.streamlit.app)**

### 2. [C++ Fundamentals](https://github.com/dayonazain-cmd/cpp-fundamentals)
> A comprehensive collection of C++ learning materials from basic to advanced levels.
*   **Key Features:** 14 organized folders covering Variables, Loops, Functions, Pointers, OOP, STL, and Mini Projects. Includes commented code for easy learning.
*   **Tech Stack:** C++, Git.

### 3. [Time Series Forecasting: SARIMA Penumpang](https://github.com/dayonazain-cmd/analisis-sarima-penumpang)
> Forecasting passenger volume using Seasonal ARIMA in R. Analyzes seasonal trends and provides accurate time-series predictions.

### 4. [Machine Learning: Random Forest Padi 2025](https://github.com/dayonazain-cmd/analisis-random-forest-padi-2025)
> Predictive modeling of rice production in Indonesia. Utilizes ensemble learning to identify key agricultural factors.

### 5. [Statistical Analysis: Kebiasaan Belajar](https://github.com/dayonazain-cmd/analisis-inferensial-kebiasaan-belajar)
> Hypothesis testing and Monte Carlo simulation on student study habits. Focuses on rigorous inferential statistics.
